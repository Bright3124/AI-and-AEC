About Me

I am a structural engineering designer focused on bridging the gap between traditional architectural and engineering practices and the next generation of AI tools. My work revolves around project design, coordination meetings, and finding ways to work smarter, not harder.

From sizing wood posts and designing reinforced concrete shear walls to managing complex code requirements (ASCE, ACI, AISC), I leverage AI and automation tools like Microsoft Power Automate to streamline workflows, allowing for more focus on critical design analysis.

AEC Use Cases & Projects

Structural Engineering Assistant

A custom AI agent configured specifically for structural designers. It acts as a rapid knowledge base for code compliance, specifically trained to reference ASCE 7 and ACI 318.

Key Feature: The agent is programmed with a priority directive to first check internal resources before utilizing broader data. Test validation included specific prompts such as querying for minimum slab reinforcement limits.
